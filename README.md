# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Super Store Sales Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes sales performance, profit, customer purchasing behavior, shipping methods, payment modes, product categories, and regional performance. It enables business users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard provides an easy-to-understand overview of business performance with dynamic filtering and drill-down capabilities.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Features

- Interactive dashboard with dynamic filters
- Region slicer (Central, East, South, West)
- KPI cards for Sales, Profit, and Quantity
- Monthly Sales Trend (Year-over-Year)
- Monthly Profit Trend (Year-over-Year)
- Sales by Payment Mode
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Ship Mode
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Product Sub-Category
- Sales and Profit by State (Map Visualization)
- Cross-filtering between visuals
- Professional dashboard layout and design

# 📈 Business Insights

### Overall Performance
- Total Sales reached approximately **$1.6M**.
- Total Profit generated was approximately **$175K**.
- Around **22K** products were sold.

### Payment Mode Analysis
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most preferred payment method.
- Online payments contribute a significant portion of total sales.
- Card payments also represent an important share of transactions.

### Regional Analysis
- The West region contributes the highest sales.
- The East region is the second-highest contributor.
- The South region has the lowest sales performance.

### Customer Segment Analysis
- Consumer customers generate the highest revenue.
- Corporate customers are the second-largest customer segment.
- Home Office contributes the smallest share of sales.

### Shipping Analysis
- Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping method.
- Second Class is the second preferred shipping option.
- Same Day shipping has the lowest usage.

### Product Category Analysis
- Office Supplies generate the highest sales.
- Technology is the second-best performing category.
- Furniture also contributes significantly to overall sales.

### Product Sub-Category Analysis
- Phones are the highest-selling sub-category.
- Chairs generate strong sales.
- Binders are another top-performing product group.

### Sales Trend
- Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, indicating seasonal business trends.
- Some months experience significantly higher sales than others.

### Profit Trend
- Profit follows a similar pattern to sales.
- Seasonal demand has a noticeable impact on profitability.

### Geographic Analysis
- Sales are distributed across multiple states.
- Some states generate significantly higher sales and profit, helping identify high-performing markets.
